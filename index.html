Our Story

Bungoma Health Link Forum was registered as a community-based organization (CBO) on 30/03/2022 with certificate no: 06937. We are located in Kanduyi Subcounty, Bungoma County. We recently launched our organization on 12/01/2024. We are a modification of a clubhouse concept where adults living with depression, bipolar, and schizophrenia are guided and supported to achieve recovery.

We refer to persons living with depression, bipolar, and schizophrenia as members and not as patients. Our membership is voluntary and timeless. We aim at combating the consequences associated with serious mental illness by supporting the strengths of our members and addressing obstacles in real time.

We provide psychiatric consultations, psychological guidance, and meals at no cost to our members. We have a staff establishment of seven (7) staff, 5 full-time and 2 part-time staff, and a board that oversees the operations at the facility.

Our Sponsor

Bungoma Health Link Forum is a community-based organization established in 2019 and registered in 2022. The organization was created to address the challenges faced by individuals living with mental health conditions in Bungoma County, with a strong focus on promoting mental health and well-being.

Thanks to the generous sponsorship of organizations like Global Action for Mental Health, whose founders are: Pauline Nicholls, Kenneth Dudek, Jeffrey Arons, David Kapten, Kinga Jedrzejczak, Dr. Ralph Aquilla, Cyrus Napolitano and David Malatesta, we are working together to tackle mental health challenges by sharing knowledge, resources, and expertise, ultimately creating a robust support system for those in need.
